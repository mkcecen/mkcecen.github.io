Game progress on your device

Levels, stars, coins, boosters, cosmetic items, daily rewards, purchase entitlements, settings and your chosen pilot name are saved on your device. The game does not require an account and does not provide its own cloud-save or public leaderboard service. The pilot name is used inside the game. Device or app-store backups may retain local data according to your platform settings.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob for banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or performance data. This supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service reliability. Personalized advertising depends on applicable consent requirements, platform permissions and provider settings. Declining tracking permission does not prevent playing the game. Rewarded ads are optional; the reward is granted when the video is completed.

Your privacy choices

Where applicable, consent choices are presented through Google's consent forms. If the provider requires a privacy-options entry, you can reopen it from the game's Settings menu. You can also manage tracking or advertising permissions in your device settings. A change of consent applies to future processing and does not undo lawful prior processing. For the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland, consent requirements apply to advertising as described by the relevant providers.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play using Unity IAP. These providers may handle transaction identifiers, product identifiers, receipts and technical information needed to process purchases and restore eligible entitlements. The game does not receive your full payment-card details. Purchase history and refunds are managed by the store. Removing ads removes banner and interstitial ads; you may still choose to watch rewarded ads.

Campaign completion notices

When a player completes the final campaign level in the installed version, the release build sends the final level number, app version and a random completion-event ID to Google Apps Script to email the developer. Different player saves generate separate alerts; the same event ID is reused on retries. The ID is specific to this completion, not an advertising or hardware identifier. No pilot name, player email address or full saved progress is sent. Google receives normal connection data such as the IP address under its privacy policy. The script keeps the event ID, level and delivery time to prevent duplicate emails. Records older than 30 days are removed when new alerts are processed. This is a developer alert, not a public leaderboard.

Support requests

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer the request, investigate problems and meet legal obligations. Do not send passwords, private API keys, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information. When reporting a problem, your app version, device model and a description are usually sufficient.

Providers and international processing

Google, Unity and your app store may process information according to their own privacy policies, including in countries other than your own. The support website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical access logs for hosting and security. We do not operate a separate gameplay analytics or account server in this version. Advertising and purchase SDKs may still process their own technical, advertising or transaction data. Ad mediation may include Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize and Meta Audience Network.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on the device until it is reset or removed with the app; platform backups may persist separately. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. Advertising, hosting and store providers set their own retention periods. Contact us for a privacy request; requests relating to provider-held data may also need to be made to that provider. Without a game account, we cannot identify every device record from an email address alone.

Request data deletion

Email mkcecen@gmail.com with the subject "Space Shuttle Sort - data deletion" and describe the data you want deleted. We will identify the developer-held support or campaign-completion records concerned; provider-held advertising and store records may require a request to that provider. Do not send passwords or payment-card information. Remove local progress by deleting the app data or uninstalling the app; backups must be managed separately. Data that must be retained for legal obligations may be kept.

Rights in your region

Depending on your location and the conditions of applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or a copy of personal data; withdraw consent; object to or restrict certain processing; or complain to a privacy authority. Relevant protections may include those in the EEA and UK, California and other US states, Brazil, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan and Hong Kong. These rights are not identical in every region. Use the available privacy choices or contact us to discuss an applicable request.
